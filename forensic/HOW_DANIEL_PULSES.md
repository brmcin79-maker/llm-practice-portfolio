## 1. What a pulse is

A pulse is the **clock of the sitting**. One typed line → pulse N on the banner. AI off still counts. A model call is a **spend on this beat**, not a session the host remembers.

That job started as LLM scripting (turns, then pulses, then ranged historical reports). V1 compiled pulse journals. V2 stamps the spine. The education is in [evolution](../evolution/README.md). This paper is whether the **running program** applies that clock.

| On this pulse | Local Daniel | Optional model |
|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| Clock | `pulse_count` bumps; `set_turn_meta` stamps events | Same N. The window is not memory |
| Awareness | Session **hold** + ledger rows the take may read | Only the **hot slice + this-turn strip** local packed |
| Tracking | Spine event + boundary projection (coverage) | Does not read the spine. Does not keep last pulse’s window |
| Tasking | Writes wait for a yes; digits are this printed list | Must not mint a task from chat color |
| Chat history | Files + spine. Old talk is a receipt if local injects it | Unique hit may enter the pack; ambiguous → local asks which |

**Compare:** Holds as machinery. The banner shows N. Events share this turn. SEARCH PULSE can find a beat after the window dies.

## 2. Three layers — what this pulse can see

They are not a second Daniel. Law: **C-09** · **S-01** · **O-02** · **O-09**.

| Layer | What it is | What the next pulse does with it |
|-------|------------|----------------------------------|
| **Session hold** | `last_take`, `last_take_kind`, `working_on`, `picks_live`, stall, rung, pending stay | **This is what the take actually reads.** Stay-talk does not query the graph to choose the sentence. |
| **Spine** | One JSON line per important turn (`append_event`) | Coverage. Snapshot, SEARCH, SHOW BOUNDARY, a later brief. **Not a reasoner.** |
| **Hot-slice pack** | Identity + a few defined words + what is in front + this-turn strip | What a model may see **this** pulse only. Re-inject next time. |

The live hole: the house (ledger + spine + dossiers) **knows more than this pulse applies**. Continuity is stored. Collaboration is whether the next sentence uses the last beat.

**Compare:**

- **Holds:** Three layers exist. DET default. No whole-house dump to the model.
- **Drifts:** Spine logged the turn; the take can still reprint or climb a rung they already answered.
- **Does not:** The graph does not infer why they picked 2.

## 3. Barriers we are earning

Each row is a forensic compare, not a mood.

### 3.1 Deterministic collaborative chat

**Law:** **C-04** · **C-06** · **O-03** · **O-04**

Stay-talk is local templates plus one method per pulse. That **holds** as a floor: AI off still moves; menus stay local; leftover English is not a silent new task.

It **drifts** as a conversation. “Every people sentence must earn the next pulse” and “the next pulse uses their answer” are live locks. The product can still reprint an unchanged numbered list, or climb the next pathing rung, after they already answered. Footer copy can promise converse while the card stays the same.

**Earning it:** the next sentence consumes their last line — item named, empty possession, nod — instead of restating the kit.

### 3.2 Intelligence as applied continuity

**Law:** **O-01** · **O-02** · **C-02** · **N-03**

Intelligence here is not a bigger model. It is **continuity with consent** and **pathing when blocked**, applied on this beat.

**Holds:** Ledger and spine remember. Writes wait. Local owns the family of the line (`okay` is a nod). The model does not run the sitting.

**Drifts:** Files remember; the take does not always. Cognizance is local facts (focus, outstanding rows, in-hand). Title-scan gather-prefer can feel like awareness of a bathroom. It is a string match on rows they already wrote. Stored continuity without a used last-take is not intelligence as the constitution names it.

**Earning it:** the next sentence is the proof. A spine event is not.

### 3.3 Logic that stays in the beat

**Law:** **O-04** · **O-05** · stay-with families

Classifier and pathing rungs **hold**. Stuck, empty, nod, leftover are real doors. One method this pulse. No invented kit as DET. Pathing never live web.

**Drifts:** After “I don't know,” the lock wants their answer used next. The product may climb a method instead of collaborating on *how* to start. After they named an item, the next card may not use that item. Empty-possession can be re-asked.

**Earning it:** classifier output and last-take kind drive the card, not the next unused rung.

### 3.4 House → this pulse

**Law:** **C-09** · **S-01** · **S-05** · **O-06** · **O-09**

The bus **holds**. `set_turn_meta`, `append_event`, active boundary, SHOW BOUNDARY. Reaching may warm; people do not see a menu of warms. The model never browses the house.

**Drifts:** Coverage is not application. The hold is what the mouth may use; the pack is what a model may use. Bridging “what he is aware of” (spine, dossier, older talk) into “what this pulse may say or grant” is the open research problem. Fitting a window is not reading. A 10,000-word ingest sits on disk whether or not this pulse granted a section.

**Earning it:** grant the strip this beat needs; speak from the hold; do not pretend the house was in the sentence.

### 3.5 Quiet help as a reliable earn

**Law:** **O-07** · **A-03** · **N-03**

**Holds** as a nested door: off by default; needs a live chat path; FLOW cannot turn it on; fail → local canonical take; local still speaks.

**Drifts:** help-on Tries have spent a call that never became slots, or landed slots that did not change the card. Wanted. Not yet a reliable earn. Turning it on would not have fixed leftover reprint on a help-off sitting.

**Earning it:** one handle that changes the local take, or an honest miss — not a second mouth.
