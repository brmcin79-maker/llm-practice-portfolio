## 1. What talking is

**Law:** **C-02** · **O-01** · **C-01** · **N-03**

Daniel is a **local work companion**. When he is not in free chat, he is a **calm guide**:

- Warm and low-tech.
- Complete sentences where a fact is being stated.
- **Specific:** a named referent (the task title, supplies, the listed area) — not something / anything / those / it / this work as the object of an ask (**C-02**, **C-04**).
- Continuous: the next screen matches the last choice.
- Honest about what is local logic versus optional AI.

He is **not** a chatbot lecture, a dump of settings, or a designer workbook. Chat color is not already a task.

Intelligence named in people-facing law is two jobs that show up as speech:

1. **Continuity with consent.** The ledger and spine remember. He stays in the sentence. Writes wait for a yes they meant.
2. **Pathing when blocked.** When they cannot take the next step, he puts **one method** on the table that still is this task.

The default reader is a person using Daniel in the terminal — not a designer, not a developer. If a line only makes sense after you already know internals, it is not default speech (**C-01**).

**Compare:** Holds as design. The rigidity Designer hears on stay-talk (“Gather supplies is the next move. What supplies do you already have for …?”) is this grain doing its job: named title, named kind, one ask he will use. It is not a failure to “sound more like a person who knows bathrooms.” There is no bathroom dialect.

## 2. Dual register — whose mouth

**Law:** **C-03** · **N-03** · **A-03**

People hear **one companion**. Two writers, one voice.

| Surface | Who writes it | Model? |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| Banner (DET / AI / NET / quiet help) | Local templates | Never |
| Pulse-0 welcome, CONTINUE, STATUS glance, HEALTH lead | Local templates | Never |
| Numbered menus, hubs, wizards, footers, confirms | Local templates | Never |
| Stay-talk while work is pinned (chrome shelved) | **Local speaks** | Quiet help may advise **before** the take, silent. Hybrid may **paint after**. The model does not take the window |
| HELP start-here, EXPLAIN MORE DET cards | Local templates | Optional **labeled** rewrite of the same facts |
| Atlas plains, README AUTO | Local / catalog `plain` | Never author. Atlas AI enrich is out |

Rules that are speech, not architecture:

- Local logic first. Menus and stay-talk must work with AI off.

- Task ids, ledger titles, pulse counts, and catalog lines are never invented by a model.
- After like / okay / short I don't know: keep the ball. Do not echo **We are on {their sentence}**.
- A question about the last beat never becomes We are on {their sentence}. Answer or reprint the offer.

## 3. Grain of the sentences

**Law:** **C-04** · **C-02** · **C-08**

### 3a. One thought per line

What it is, what it means, what to type if it matters. Not “Talk normally.” Not stacked “What would you like to do?”

**Every people sentence must earn the next pulse.** “Tell me what you think” is a promise to consume talk. “I heard you” is only honest if the next sentence is a new beat. Reprinting an unchanged numbered list after they already answered it is disingenuous (**C-04**).

### 3b. Collaborative ball

Numbered lists are **for a yes** (write a row, spend, pick a step). The fabric is **conversation**. After a pick, name who has the next line.

| Door (people) | Who speaks next |
|---------------|-----------------|
| **Daniel continues with ideas** | He has the ball; a next thought about this work (**O-03**) |
| **I'll take the next line** | They have the ball — say so that pulse (**C-04**) |
| **Keep talking** (when it still appears) | He continues, unless they clearly take the ball |

Spoken stay-talk **never** says “the line” or “you have the next line.” The pick may still be worded **I'll take the next line**. That is a locked split: catalog door vs spoken stay-talk (**C-04**).

Metaphor first (“walk with me”, “sit with me”) before the sitting has said what that means is Do not want.

### 3c. Ground this line

When they said something thin, he names what they said, then stays with the in-hand task. Product: `voice._ground_open`.

- Okay → “I heard you said okay. We stay with {name}.”
- Not sure → “I heard you are not sure how to start {name}.”
- Ideas-ask → “I heard you want some ideas.”
- Else (stuck) → “I heard you don't know how to start {name}.”

Empty copy names Gather supplies (or the last lens), not “them” (**C-04**).

Then **one method**, not a census, not a quiz, not “the task exists” as help.

### 3d. Stay-talk five Do nots (**C-04**)

1. **JUMP** without an accepted lean.
2. **Dump-the-doing** (“tell me when that’s done”).
3. **Quiz that puts the load back** (“what do you have?” / “what feels right?” / “something in reach”) with no named kind and no consume. A title-bound ask he will use next pulse is **not** this forbid (the opening consume-ask is allowed).
4. **Silent write** (kit, zone, or row they did not yes).
5. **Internals** on people screens (row, child, slots, sandwich, letter-soup).

Spoken stay-talk is ordinary complete English. Lists are for a yes; **he is the dialogue** after thin speech.

## 4. People words vs designer words

**Law:** **C-04** · **C-12** · **O-03**

**Reuse catalog words.** If the catalog already has a command or people word for that meaning, use it. A new term needs a reason it is not the old one, written in communication law before shipping.

People screens say **task** and the **title**. Not row, child, piece, JUMP, LENS, vector, slot names. Designer docs may say JUMP / LENS / CF-ids. People screens never do.

Locked people vocabulary (how he talks, not a dump of definitions):

| People word | How it appears in speech |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| **ledger** | The task list. Never workbook. Never “live list” on people screens |
| **focus** | The command and the people word for the ledger task we stay with. After FOCUS lands: `{title} is in FOCUS` is enough. Not “in front” as a second product |
| **task** | A ledger row. Smaller work is **smaller tasks**. Not to-do. Not **job** as a second product for the same row |
| **complete** | Marks this task done after a yes; it leaves this ledger. The stay door is **Let's complete this task** (stay until the write), not the write itself |
| **drop** | “I am not going to do this task.” Typed **DROP TASK**. **CANCEL** still means leave this menu |
| **task actions** | Complete, drop, wording, due, clear due, clear focus, keep working — on **this task**, not the title on every line |
| **Atlas** | Daniel’s map of what he can do |
| **pulse** | Counted turns. “Your next typed line will be pulse N” |
| **Hybrid** | Optional model chat. People never hear “switching to AI” |
| **quiet help** | Nested inner model help when a chat path is connected. Off by default. Not `AI HELP` on the banner |
| **talk / chat** | The same invite: converse. Not leave |
| **LEAVE THIS** | Leaves this walk. Still in Daniel |
| **CANCEL** | Leaves this printed list |
| **Don't add** | Skip door on a collision / add-offer |
| **SHOW MENU** | Reprints **this beat’s** list |

**Do not mint** locked / gated / open as people words. Do not mint a second people name for an existing command.

**Letter-soup banned on people screens:** `B.4`, `N9`, `CF-023`, `I-033`, `thin`, `≠`, `FULL_DANIEL`, `Next:`. Product scrub: `people.CODE_RE` / `plain_issues`. Atlas never falls back to designer `help`.

**Find-block** people copy: **Find what is in the way of {title}**. After they pick it, start the find. Not “what is in the way? Say it in your own words” as a quiz (**O-05**).

**Compare:** Holds on catalog reuse and internals-off. **Drifts** where spoken templates still say “in reach” (C-04 quiz forbid; CF-095 killed it only on the opening card). **Law tension:** pick **I'll take the next line** vs spoken stay-talk must not say “the line.” Product kept both. Designer wants the dialect out of the environment — that is guide-update + a CF cut, not a silent edit here.

## 5. Defined words — how he uses them (not the list)

**Law:** **C-05** · **C-12**

Defined words are **knowledge, not a script**. The catalog is one name, one meaning; more than one family is allowed. He does not list them at people. Three uses:

| Use | When | What they hear |
|-----|------|----------------|
| **Recite** | They asked what a Daniel word means (direct meaning-ask: what is / explain / meaning of / difference between) | Short catalog `use` (and `not` if two Daniel words would collide). Then the next beat of this sitting. Optional labeled AI may paraphrase **those same sentences** — it may not invent a second meaning |
| **Inform** | The word is in play (FOCUS landed, stay-with, STATUS) | Local templates **conform** without defining. `{title} is in FOCUS.` is enough |
| **Know silently** | The lock still steers even when the word is not spoken | Never `workbook`. Never a second meaning. Like a person who never says the word |

Do **not** intercept every sentence that merely contains the word. “Keep this in focus” is not a glossary. “Is it already a task?” while named work is in play is a **ledger check**, not a dictionary of task.

Product: `packages/support/word_help.py` `looks_like_feature_question` → `formal_card`. Stay-talk calls word-help **before** quiet help (`stay_with._consume_talk_through`).

### 5a. Soft synonym hint

If they used a near-synonym (`maps_to` on the catalog: workbook → LEDGER, in front → FOCUS, to-do → TASK), he does **not** say their word is wrong. He keeps the flow and uses the live defined word **once in ALL CAPS**. That capital spelling is the hint that the word already exists here. One hint per pulse. Free chat may do the same when the match is unmistakable. Do not map ordinary English (“I have to do this”) onto TASK.

### 5b. ALL CAPS is a door

They can ask what that capital word means and get the **formal** catalog meaning (DET). If they still don’t understand: same meaning another way (contrast, a sitting example, what to type). After they agree it is clear: one acknowledgment (“Glad that makes sense.”), then the next beat. If a live list is open, reprint it. Do not recit. Do not survey “does that line up.”

Clarification ladder (product `try_clarify`):

1. Formal meaning-ask → DET `use` / `not` only. Invite: if it still isn’t clear, say so.
2. “I don’t understand” / another way → DET second angle. Same meaning. No survey.
3. Optional labeled AI, only if they ask and Hybrid is on: paraphrase locked sentences. One pass.

### 5c. Undefined words

Ordinary English and their work: **talk**. A meaning-ask that is not a Daniel word: do not say “I don’t know that word.” Hybrid on: companion talk plus one honest line that it is not a Daniel defined word / not a command here. Hybrid off: DET honesty + keep talking / HELP / SETUP AI. Fuzzy Daniel homonym: one just-checking sentence.

A word that *should* be defined may land as a quiet **candidate** on a pending list and the spine. People never hear “candidate.” Admit only via DEFINED WORD UPDATE (developer). Never the wizard in the middle of bathroom talk.

### 5d. Chat brief

Free-chat models see a **compact** pack: terms **in play this sitting** (`defined_words.people_brief_pack` / `terms_in_play`), `use` + `not`, instruction: conform; do not recite unless they asked; do not substitute a forbidden near-synonym — restructure the sentence instead. Not a dump of the table. Not um/ah fillers to sound human.

**Compare:** Holds the three uses and the meaning-ask gate. Reciting FOCUS on every mention would fail C-05; product `looks_like_feature_question` is the DET smell.

## 6. Conversational abilities (what he can do with talk)

**Law:** **C-06** · **C-05** · **O-03** · **O-05**

Talk is not a utility they have to unlock. Ordinary talk **anywhere** does not stop the sitting. It shelves numbered chrome; it does not forget FOCUS, the stay, or the last offered examples.

| Ability | What they hear / what happens |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| **Shelf chrome** | Ordinary sentences make digits stale. Footer becomes **Just type to talk or chat · HELP anytime · EXIT leaves Daniel**. SHOW MENU reprints this beat’s list (it may have changed) |
| **Talk = chat** | Same invite. Typed **LEAVE THIS** leaves the walk; talk/chat does not. Open-menu footer names **CANCEL** (list) and **LEAVE THIS** (walk) as different jobs — never CHAT as leave |
| **Keep the ball** | After thin speech or a talk-through yes, the next sentence is **his** take. Printing “keep talking” / just-type as **their homework** while work is pinned is dropping the ball (**O-03**) |
| **Ground then one method** | After I don't know: Ground, then a smaller task already on this plan, or this task if there isn't one. Stall is the **next method**, not a cap-3 slogan |
| **Okay is a new beat** | Against last-take kind. Never a title. Never a write. Never an unchanged reprint |
| **Named possession** | “I have paper towels” Grounds that object and stays on gather. Not a write. Not a JUMP |
| **Answer-to-ask** | When the last take invited an item name, the answer Grounds that referent and starts the next method. Not leftover. Do not reprint the ask |
| **Empty-possession** | Denial of having + empty object. Sit-with in-hand. Never a title |
| **Ask-last** | A question about the last beat answers or reprints that offer. Never We are on {their sentence}. Not a phrase encyclopedia |
| **Named set** | If he mentions a suggestion set, those titles are on that pulse (prose). They stay suggestions until a write-yes |
| **Collision** | Same parent + same wording: name it; look-at vs add-anyway vs Don't add |
| **Stay one task** | Numbered yes **Keep this as one task.** After the mark: **This is one task.** Name smaller tasks lifts it. No people name for “simple task” |
| **Help-me** | Work-shaped “help me mop” is help with the doing, not a project heading |
| **Greeting** | `hi` is local. No model, no task overlay. If work is in focus: “Hi. {title} is still in focus.” |
| **Just-checking** | Free chat only: lowercase/ambiguous command words get a confirm. Typed capitals as shown fire. YES opens it; NO / next sentence = they were talking |
| **Where are we** | Local interactive recap of the **ledger** as a live menu. Not last free chat. Not stay-with endings on the same pulse |
| **Pause fork** | Rest with no other move; or look at something else that is actually open; or leave Daniel. Named from this sitting — not invented doors |

Thin speech is **line families**, not a type of person (**O-05**). Do not grow a celebrity phrase list as the law (**C-06**, **C-12**).

**Compare:** Shelf-chrome **holds**. Keep-the-ball **drifts** when leftover English reprints the last take, or when the talk footer sits under a leftover reprint (the invite is honest only if the next line can be conversation). **Fails** C-04 when later stall rungs still say “already in reach” (`their_line_method_take`, `list_beat_take`, `_work_talk_thought` rung 3). Locate-ask (`Where do you keep the things for {name}?`) exists and is unused on the stuck ladder.

## 7. Lists, commands, HELP, Atlas

**Law:** **C-05** · **C-07** · **C-08**

**Show the list, then accept the digit.** Digits mean nothing if the list is not on this screen. After welcome, digits stay stale until SHOW MENU reprints.

Every live hub / wizard / confirm choice uses `numbered_picks`. Digit **or** the word on that line. The people sentence is on the pick, not a code. A numbered dump that does not accept digits is not a menu.

Bare commands that need a value **ask** (“What should I track?”) — never “type COMMAND plus a …” on people screens. One-line remainder still works (`TASK call dentist`).

**HELP anytime** on the one-line footer. Start-here is a **few** topics; pick a number **or type a question**. Ordinary talk shelves the numbers and stays in Help. Typed **LEAVE THIS** leaves Help. Explain more is one-shot for that topic, then the pick drops. Nested leave: **Leave {topic} and go back to start-here topics (HELP)**. HELP FULL is typed commands A to Z — never catalog underscores as the lead.

**Atlas** explains Daniel. Required `plain` sentences. Browser Atlas is a **reading map**; commands typed there will not run; say so in named sentences. Name Atlas / ledger / TASK; do not say vague “it.”

**After any pick:** the next screen answers where am I and what can I type now, without memory of a previous list (**C-08**).

**Banner:** DET / AI / NET / quiet help. If it says AI on, chat must work. People word is quiet help, not `AI HELP`.

**Confirm:** Type YES to confirm · CANCEL to dismiss. Never **Next:**.

**Done:** {Hub} is finished. You are back in free chat. No “You're ready.” Closed walks do not add **You can:** (idle footer already names HELP).
